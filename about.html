About Roamly

Roamly is a comprehensive travel and tourism platform designed to connect travellers with the breathtaking beauty, rich culture, and vibrant industries of Sri Lanka.

Originally conceived as a capstone project for the ICT 2022 Full Stack Development Project, Roamly has grown into a highly interactive, community-driven ecosystem. Our goal is to streamline the travel experience by offering unified maps, seamless booking directories, dynamic galleries, and critical emergency support—all wrapped in a premium, accessible user interface.

Key Features

🗺️

Interactive Mapping

Explore all 25 districts of Sri Lanka with our custom-built, highly responsive SVG map interfaces.

📅

Dynamic Bookings

A fully-featured directory for Transports, Guides, Hotels, and Restaurants with real-time sorting capabilities.

✨

Immersive UI/UX

Built with modern frontend standards featuring seamless Dark Mode integration and stunning scrolling animations.

🚨

Emergency Hub

A one-tap emergency contact directory ensuring tourist safety and peace of mind across the island.
